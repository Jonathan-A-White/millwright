symptom: An example word shown to teach a letter or a sound does not make that sound where it is marked (τοῖς and ὀφθαλμοῖς for ο 'as in lot', though ο is in the pair οι)
cause: Examples are picked by the letter's spelling, not by the sound the rig's one pronunciation scheme gives it in that word (a vowel pair, a digraph, a consonant that softens before e or i)
sweep: Every sheet, card or drill that shows example words for a sound, over every text the rig holds: each example's marked letters, read by the one pronunciation scheme, make the sound taught
kinds: phonics
since: 2026-10-14
