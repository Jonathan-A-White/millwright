# Vision

This is the Governor's own to write; the Mayor consults it when weighing competing epics. Keep it short: it is read at every boot.

**What <the factory> is for.**
*One or two sentences, in the Governor's own words, on what this factory is for and for whom.*

**What matters, in order.**
*A numbered list of what matters most, so competing epics can be weighed against each other. Typical entries: fuel (what you can spend, and the knobs to scale it down and up); that work continues while you do not; that you steer per story (rig, branch, model, effort, formula); few moving parts, with dampers before speed; truthful records, no blame.*

**Order of work.**
*What gets built first, second, third: the factory itself, then its first customer, then the rest.*
