## FIRST

The numbered things the successor does before anything else, most urgent first: what to arm, what waits on the Governor's answer (the bead, the card, what to do on each answer), what is half done and where it is written down. Each is an action, not a story.

## What he decided

Each decision of the Governor since the last handoff, in his words where you have them, with the bead it is recorded on. 'Nothing new this sitting' is a fine entry. Never record a decision you made for him; label a provisional choice *provisional, Governor to confirm*.

## Where things stand

What is running, what has landed and been verified, what is held and why, what is blocked on what, and every hands step still owed, each with its bead id. Say what you checked and when, not what you assume.

## What is new

Anything about the machinery, the seats or the Governor's habits that the successor would otherwise learn the hard way, one line each. Anything that is a standing rule moves to `procedures.md` instead.
