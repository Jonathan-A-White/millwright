# A talk is one resumed session, within the hour; a story is one fresh session

A story is worked by a fresh session (ADR 0002): its continuity lives in the seat and the beads, and a context that grows is a cost with no return. A talk is the opposite case. An app conversation with the tutor (a Lampas bible-talk, later SpellForge's and Legend's tutors) has its continuity nowhere but in the conversation itself; re-sending the last ten turns raw each time (lampas talk.ts, 8000-byte cap) gives the tutor a ten-turn memory and the talk wanders. So the mill keeps one Claude session per talk, named by the app (`talk` in the grist envelope), and resumes it every turn by session id with the app's history stripped. The prompt cache (one-hour TTL, set on every talk grist) makes the resumed prefix cheap: a cache write once per session, reads every turn.

A session lives only within the hour's cache (the Governor's answer B, 2026-10-21, mw-pxji7y): a turn that comes an hour or more after the last one is never resumed; it starts a fresh session whose memory is the app's own running summary of the talk and its last turns, so an uncached prefix is never paid. Within the hour, the limit is in context: at the context cap (80000 tokens by default) or the turn cap (40) the session writes its key points, ends, and the next turn starts fresh from them; the app keeps the key points as the talk's summary. One grist at a time per talk, since two resumes of one session interleave its transcript. A spec change, a billing change or a lost transcript also starts fresh with the app's history as the fallback, and says so.

## Consequences

The app's history and summary stay in the input, as the fallback and the fresh session's memory. The mill holds state per talk (a record under its state dir, a transcript under the harness's project dir on the home) and tidies both; a talk lives on one host, the home. The warm pool is for talk-less grists. A talk's cost grows with its length up to the cap (about 2x a fresh turn at the cap) and is read per turn from the grist's fuel line. The eval and the smoke can run a two-turn example and end the talks they start.
